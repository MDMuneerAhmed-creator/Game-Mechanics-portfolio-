01 / About

Who I Am

I'm Mohammad Muneer Ahmed, a third-year Computer Science undergraduate at SR University, Warangal. This portfolio showcases my work for the Game Mechanics subject — a hands-on exploration of game design and development using Unity.

Through this subject, I've built 8 individual Unity games — each focused on a distinct mechanic: movement, physics, AI logic, puzzle systems, input handling, and more. Every project is a step deeper into understanding what makes games feel alive.

I also led a team project, JetClutch — a 3D fighter jet game — where I coordinated a team of 6 to bring a fully realised 3D experience to life.

TEAM_PROJECT_01

JetClutch

A 3D action game developed in Unity where the player controls a fighter jet navigating through an open environment. The gameplay focuses on basic flight movement — forward motion, turning, and altitude control — while avoiding obstacles and maintaining stability. Demonstrates 3D object control, camera follow systems, physics-based movement, and real-time environment interaction.
